# 🎬 Aegis — Demo Video Script (≤ 3 minutes)

> Follows the official guidelines: intro to problem → solution walkthrough → product demo → tech explanation → future scope & impact.
> Record screen capture (OBS/Loom/ScreenStudio) + voiceover. Add captions (many judges watch muted). Keep it ≤ 3:00. Tight and emotional beats long and thorough.

**Tools:** OBS or ScreenStudio for capture · CapCut/DaVinci Resolve for editing · clean voiceover (record separately, sync after) · royalty-free subtle background music, low volume.

---

## Shot list & script

### [0:00–0:20] — The hook (problem, emotional)
**Visual:** Black screen → text fades in over a phone-ringing sound. Then your face cam OR a stock shot of an older person on the phone.
**VO:**
> "This is a phone call. The voice belongs to her grandson. He's scared, he's in trouble, he needs money now. There's just one problem — it's not him. It's an AI clone of his voice, made from three seconds of audio. This happens every five minutes, and last year it cost families over twelve billion dollars."

**On-screen captions:** `$40B in AI fraud by 2027` · `3 seconds to clone a voice` · `70% can't tell it's fake`

---

### [0:20–0:35] — The gap + the introduction
**Visual:** Cut to the Aegis landing page. Logo animates in.
**VO:**
> "Banks catch fraud after the money's gone. Spam filters miss AI scams completely. We built something that steps in *during* the attack. This is Aegis — an AI guardian that stops scams before they happen."

---

### [0:35–2:05] — The demo (the rescue — the heart of the video)
**Visual:** The guardian dashboard. Click **"Simulate live call."** The scam transcript streams in line by line.

**VO (narrate as it happens):**
> "Here's that call, live. As it unfolds, Aegis's Scam DNA Engine reads the conversation in real time."

**Visual:** The **risk meter** starts climbing. **Tactic cards** pop in one by one.
**VO:**
> "Watch. It catches the fake emergency. The impersonation of a family member. The demand for secrecy — 'don't tell mom.' And the irreversible wire transfer. These are the four signatures of a scam — and Aegis doesn't look for keywords, it understands the manipulation itself. That's why it works on any call, text, or chat, in any language."

**Visual:** Risk meter hits red (96). **Verdict banner: DANGER.** Plain-language summary appears.
**VO:**
> "Risk: 96. And here's where Aegis is different — it doesn't just warn. It acts."

**Visual:** **Trusted Circle** notification fires — show the phone mockup / real SMS arriving: *"⚠️ Aegis detected a likely scam targeting Margaret. Call her now."*
**VO:**
> "It instantly alerts a trusted family member — the one thing proven to stop elder fraud in the real world."

**Visual:** **Transaction Shield** panel: the pending **$4,000 transfer flips to "HELD BY AEGIS."**
**VO:**
> "And it freezes the payment — before a single dollar leaves the account. Margaret keeps her money. Her daughter calls the real grandson, who's safe at home."

**Visual (optional, 5s):** Paste a *benign* message → verdict "Safe." 
**VO:** "And on a normal conversation? Aegis stays quiet. No false alarms."

---

### [2:05–2:30] — How it works (tech, fast)
**Visual:** The architecture diagram (animate the flow).
**VO:**
> "Under the hood: a large language model powers the Scam DNA Engine with structured, explainable output. Speech-to-text turns calls into text, the engine scores the manipulation, and our intervention layer fires the family alert and the transaction hold. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, and Claude — deployed and live."

---

### [2:30–3:00] — Impact + vision + close
**Visual:** Quick montage of screenshots → end card with logo + tagline + links.
**VO:**
> "AI fraud is a forty-billion-dollar problem, and it targets the people we love most. Aegis turns that moment of danger into a moment of safety — for over a billion vulnerable people worldwide. Next, we're embedding it directly into banks, phone carriers, and eldercare. Scammers got an AI. It's time the rest of us got one too."

**End card text:** `AEGIS — Stop scams before they happen.` + live URL + GitHub + "Beyond Tomorrow Summit 2026."

## Production checklist
- [ ] Total runtime ≤ 3:00 (aim 2:45).
- [ ] Captions/subtitles burned in.
- [ ] Stats appear on screen as text, not just spoken.
- [ ] Demo segment is the longest part (~90s) and runs flawlessly — re-record until clean.
- [ ] Audio is clear; music is subtle and under the voice.
- [ ] First 5 seconds hook hard (judges decide fast).
- [ ] Ends with logo, tagline, live URL, GitHub.
- [ ] Uploaded to YouTube (unlisted) or Vimeo; link works in incognito.
- [ ] Same video linked in README + Devpost.

## Recording tips
- Record the screen flow first, perfectly, *then* lay the voiceover over it — easier than live narration.
- If the live API is slow on camera, use the cached fixture response so the meter animates instantly.
- Slow down. A calm, confident pace reads as "this is real," not "this is a student project."
