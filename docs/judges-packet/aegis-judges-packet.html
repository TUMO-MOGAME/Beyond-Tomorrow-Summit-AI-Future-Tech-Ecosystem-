The Problem Why now
01 — THE PROBLEM

AI made scams infinitely scalable. Our defenses didn't keep up.

Generative AI made fraud faster, cheaper, and nearly impossible to detect — and the people who suffer most are those least equipped to fight back.

$40B
Projected US AI-enabled fraud losses by 2027 (up from $12.3B in 2023 — 32% CAGR).

Entrust
WHY TODAY'S DEFENSES FAIL
01
Reactive
Banks and existing tools flag fraud after the transfer clears. By then the money — often irreversible — is already gone.
02
Keyword-based
Blocklist and keyword filters are trivially evaded — AI-generated scams rewrite themselves on every attempt and walk straight past them.
03
No human moment
There is no intervention while the victim is being manipulated. They are alone with the attacker at the one moment that matters.

Everything today is forensics. The problem demands prevention.

The Solution Scam DNA Engine
02 — HOW IT WORKS

A real-time guardian that detects intent — and intervenes.

The Scam DNA Engine analyzes any conversation and detects the underlying manipulation structure — urgency, authority impersonation, secrecy, fear, irreversible payment — not surface keywords. When risk crosses the threshold on a money scam, Aegis acts.

88/100 risk
● Danger — Intervene
0 · Safe50 · Caution≥75 · Fire intervention
1

Ingest

Paste or simulate a live call, SMS, or chat. One pipeline, modality-agnostic.

Any channel
2

Scam DNA Engine

An LLM returns structured JSON: risk score, the exact triggering quotes, the manipulation tactics, a plain-language summary, and a recommended action.

LLM · Zod
3

Warn + alert the Trusted Circle

The user sees why it's dangerous in plain language; a pre-set family member is alerted — the most effective real-world circuit-breaker for elder fraud.

Human-in-loop
4

Freeze the payment — Transaction Shield

The suspicious transfer is held before it completes, stopping the loss at the moment it matters.

Fintech
WHY AEGIS IS DIFFERENT — THE MOAT
01
Prevention, not forensics
Acts during the attack, before the payment leaves — not after the money is gone.
02
Intent, not keywords
One engine generalizes across every channel and language. The Spanish demo proves it — no retraining.
03
Trusted Circle + Care Mode
Loops in family. The senior does nothing — family sets it up once. A social safety net, not a lonely alert.
